How it works

  1. Add your children. Give each one a name, their school year and a PIN.
  2. Set the daily targets. Choose the activities and how many correct answers make a finished day.
  3. Hand over your phone or tablet. Your child signs in with their PIN and practises until their targets are met.
  4. Check the family board. See who has finished today, and give the reward when the day is done.

The benefits

For your child
  • Practice at the right level: maths follows their year of the national curriculum, one skill at a time.
  • Moves on once a skill is secure, and brings older skills back so they are not forgotten.
  • An instant answer, with the worked solution, after every question.
  • A clear finish line each day, not an endless feed.
For you
  • No questions to prepare and no marking.
  • See what each child has mastered, and their last 14 days.
  • You decide the targets and the rewards.
  • Every question is written by hand and checked, not generated by AI.
Safe by design. No adverts, no chat, nothing shared with other families. Children sign in with a PIN on your device, and they know you can see their work.
