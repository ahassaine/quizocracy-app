How it works
  • Each child earns stars every week from their own plan: up to 10 a day for doing it, and a few more for extra practice. So a Year 1 and a Year 9 child compete fairly.
  • Your children are on one table at home. With friends, a league joins up to {{ MAX_FAMILIES }} families: the family who makes it sends its code to the others, and their grown-up types it here.
  • In a friends' league other families see only a nickname and an animal picture for each child, never a name. There is no chat and nothing anyone can type to another family.
