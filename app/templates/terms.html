Terms of use

Beta draft — to be reviewed before paid launch.

  • Accounts are for parents and guardians aged 18 or over. Children use Quizocracy through their parent's account.
  • The beta is free and may change or stop; we will tell you before anything you rely on changes.
  • Founding families. A family that signs up during the beta is a founding family: Quizocracy stays free for it, for all its children, for 12 months after paid launch begins. We will tell you the launch date at least 30 days before.
  • Inviting another family. Each family has its own invitation link (on Home), which works from now on, during the beta. When a family signs up with your link and one of its children answers questions in that family's second week (days 8 to 14 after it signs up), both families get one more free month, added after the 12 months above. A family that signs up with another code, or that you invite after it already has an account, does not count. Bonus months have no cash value and cannot be transferred.
  • Practice content follows the national curriculum in England (Years 1–9); it does not replace teaching. {{ LEFT_OUT }}
  • Please keep your password and your children's PINs private.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings.
